Description

A three-piece lawn ensemble that pairs cool, breathable fabric with delicate hand-embroidered florals along the neckline and sleeves. Cut for an easy, all-day silhouette, it’s the kind of piece that softens with every wash.

The mint-green shade is dyed in small batches at our Lahore studio, then finished with a subtle chiffon dupatta in a complementary tone — perfect for layering through Karachi summers or under a light shawl in Islamabad spring.

Includes shirt, pant and dupatta. Unstitched fabric is also available on request — reach out via WhatsApp before ordering.
