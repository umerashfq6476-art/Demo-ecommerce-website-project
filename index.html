OUR STORY

Crafted with love, worn with pride

Velvet was born in the heart of Lahore in 2018, founded on a simple belief — every Pakistani woman deserves clothing made with intention. From handpicked lawns to hand-finished embroidery, every piece passes through the careful hands of artisans who treat fabric as art.

We work with women weavers, dyers, and embroiderers across Punjab, paying fair wages and preserving craft techniques that took generations to perfect. When you wear Velvet, you wear the work of a community — and you help us empower it further.
