Our Story

Born in Lahore. Made for the world.

The Beginning

From a small studio to a movement

Velvet was founded in 2018 by Sara Ahmed in a small studio in Gulberg, Lahore. What started with just 10 carefully sketched designs has grown into a collection of over 200 silhouettes, serving customers across Pakistan and shipped to wardrobes around the world.

Every season, we return to that same studio — to the quiet hum of sewing machines, the smell of fresh fabric, and the belief that brought us here in the first place.

“ Our mission: make every Pakistani woman feel like royalty.
What We Stand For

Our Values

🧵

Quality First

We source only premium fabrics — handpicked lawns, pure chiffons, and finely woven cottons — and inspect every piece before it leaves our studio.

🌿

Sustainable

From eco-friendly packaging to low-waste cutting, we work to reduce our footprint without compromising the craft.

🤝

Community

We partner with local artisans across Punjab — weavers, dyers, embroiderers — paying fair wages and keeping generational craft alive.

Meet the Founder

Sara Ahmed

SA

Founder & Creative Director

A graduate of the Pakistan Institute of Fashion & Design, Sara spent her early years assisting Lahore’s most respected ateliers before striking out on her own. She founded Velvet with one rule — design only what she’d wear herself. Seven years on, that rule still guides every collection.
